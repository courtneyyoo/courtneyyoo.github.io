About

I'm a Vancouver-based designer and developer who loves the whole process of making digital products, from the first concept and user research through UX/UI design, development, and launch. Because I work on both sides, my designs are made to be built, and my code stays true to the design.

I've designed and built custom WordPress sites for clients, redesigned a website that was chosen and launched as the official site, and designed and developed a cross-platform mobile app. I'm a lifelong problem solver, and I care about making things simple, intuitive, and visually pleasing.
